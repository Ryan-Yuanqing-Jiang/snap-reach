# Prospect Challenges: LSKD

## Overview
LSKD is a high-growth, Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) activewear and gym clothing brand. Operations heavily rely on e-commerce, digital marketing, and community building ("1% better every day"). 

## Primary Challenges & Operations Gaps

### 1. Scaling Omnichannel Marketing & Personalization
As a major DTC apparel brand with frequent drops (e.g., Miami Collection, Power Play), LSKD needs to engage a massive customer base across email, SMS, and social media. Creating highly segmented, personalized marketing campaigns manually or with disjointed tools is a massive margin-burner and leads to lower conversion rates.

### 2. Customer Data Fragmentation
LSKD likely uses different platforms for their storefront (e.g., Shopify), customer service, and marketing. This creates data silos. Without a single, unified view of the customer, the team cannot effectively track the lifetime value (LTV) or trigger timely re-engagement campaigns (like cart abandonment or post-purchase follow-ups).

### 3. Scaling Customer Support Efficiency
With rapid growth and global sales, ticketing volume for sizing queries, returns, and shipping updates scales exponentially. Relying on manual support reduces response times and lowers customer satisfaction, directly impacting the brand experience and retention.
