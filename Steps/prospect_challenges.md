# Operational Challenge Breakdown: Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA)

**Perspective:** Chief Information Officer / Head of Engineering, Banking & Financial Services

## 1. Core Business Operations & Critical Challenges

### Software Engineering & Feature Delivery
- **The Operation:** Building and maintaining our digital banking interfaces (CommBank app, NetBank) and internal enterprise applications. We have thousands of developers pushing code to improve customer experience and ensure regulatory compliance.
- **Our Biggest Headache:** The sheer volume of "busywork"—finding context across Jira, Bitbucket, and Confluence, writing boilerplate code, and deciphering legacy systems. This burns massive engineering hours and slows down our time-to-market against agile neobanks.

### Security, Compliance & Code Quality
- **The Operation:** Rigorous code review, testing, and security scanning before any software reaches production. We operate in a highly regulated environment where vulnerabilities are unacceptable.
- **Our Biggest Headache:** Code reviews are a massive bottleneck. Senior engineers spend hours reviewing Pull Requests manually to align with strict internal guidelines and security standards. This pulls our best minds away from solving high-value architectural problems.

### Cross-Team Standardization & Compliance
- **The Operation:** Scaling our engineering organization requires absolute consistency. We have thousands of developers across disparate teams pushing code into the Commonwealth Bank ecosystem daily.
- **Our Biggest Headache:** Ensuring every team adheres consistently to our strict architectural, security, and compliance standards is incredibly difficult at scale. Relying solely on manual processes leaves room for human error and reliability issues, particularly as we integrate more AI-generated components.

## 2. ROI Prioritization for Atlassian Rovo

**1. Software Engineering & Feature Delivery: Accelerating Code Generation and Context Finding**
- **Why it's a priority:** We spend $1.6B annually on tech. We need our developers building features, not searching for documentation. Solving this accelerates our ability to release digital products that keep our customers engaged.
- **How Atlassian Rovo moves the needle:** Rovo's deep integration with the Atlassian Teamwork Graph means our developers can instantly access context from Jira and Confluence directly in their IDE. The agentic AI can generate boilerplate, run multi-step code refactoring, and translate Jira requirements into code, drastically cutting down "busywork."

**2. Security, Compliance & Code Quality: Automating Pull Request Reviews**
- **Why it's a priority:** Security is non-negotiable for a Tier-1 bank, but human bottlenecks in PR reviews delay releases and consume expensive senior talent. Removing this friction speeds up our DevSecOps pipeline.
- **How Atlassian Rovo moves the needle:** Rovo can perform first-pass code reviews, enforce our internal coding standards, and automatically validate that code matches the Jira ticket's acceptance criteria, saving massive amounts of senior engineering time.

**3. Cross-Team Standardization & Compliance: Enforcing Enterprise-Grade Governance**
- **Why it's a priority:** For a major financial institution, fragmented coding practices or a single compliance breach can result in catastrophic financial and reputational damage. Consistency and reliability are paramount.
- **How Atlassian Rovo moves the needle:** Rovo Dev can be customized with our internal coding guidelines and security boundaries to automatically enforce compliance at the point of creation. It ensures that all AI-generated or developer-written code is standardized, secure, and reliable across the entire engineering ecosystem before it even reaches a pull request.
