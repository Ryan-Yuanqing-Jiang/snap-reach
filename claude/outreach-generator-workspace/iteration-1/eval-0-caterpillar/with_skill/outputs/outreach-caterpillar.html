Understanding your world

The parts forecasting trap

Heavy equipment manufacturers juggle complexity that spreadsheets and legacy ERP systems weren't built to handle.

📦
Portfolio Complexity
Tens of thousands of part numbers across multiple equipment families, regions, and dealer tiers. Traditional forecasting treats all SKUs the same. Your fast-moving fasteners and slow-moving hydraulic systems demand completely different replenishment logic.
💰
Working Capital Trap
Over-forecast and parts languish on dealer shelves; under-forecast and you miss sales and frustrate customers. Most organizations slide toward safety stock, tying up millions in capital. Commodity price swings make it worse.
🔗
Network Visibility Gaps
Dealer inventory levels, regional demand patterns, and supplier lead times often live in silos. Manual reconciliation is slow and error-prone. By the time procurement sees demand signals, they're already historical.
The shift we enable
SupplySmart aggregates supplier lead times, historical part velocity, seasonal patterns, and macroeconomic signals into a single predictive model. Your procurement team gets forward-looking inventory targets—not backward-looking reports. You reduce safety stock, accelerate cash conversion cycles, and improve dealer satisfaction by ensuring availability. Better forecasting means better capital allocation.
