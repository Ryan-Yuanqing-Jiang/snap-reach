How RevAI Works

RevAI is an AI-powered revenue cycle management platform designed specifically for large, multi-location healthcare systems. We focus on three high-impact areas where AI delivers measurable returns:

1. Intelligent Charge Capture & Integrity

Problem: Clinical teams miss valid charges daily due to documentation gaps and workflow inefficiencies. This leakage compounds across hundreds of locations.

Our AI analyzes clinical documentation in real-time, identifying missed or under-coded charges before claims leave your system. For Mayo's scale, this typically recovers $1.5-3M annually.

2. Denial Prevention & Smart Recovery

Problem: Claims denials consume tremendous staff hours and result in lost revenue. Large systems see 5-8% denial rates.

RevAI predicts claim denials before submission and automatically corrects them. We also intelligently prioritize appeals based on likelihood of success and financial impact. Result: 40-60% reduction in denial volume, 2-3 point improvement in first-pass clean rate.

3. Payer Intelligence & Optimization

Problem: Each payer has unique requirements, timing, and approval patterns. Managing this manually is impossible at scale.

Our platform learns payer-specific behaviors and automatically optimizes claim submission strategies. We identify which denials to appeal vs. resubmit, spot systematic issues, and flag high-value cases for immediate attention.
