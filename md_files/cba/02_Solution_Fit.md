# CBA + Atlassian Rovo Dev: Strategic Partnership & Solution Fit

## 1. The Executive Gap (Status Quo vs. Future State)
CBA's massive $1.6B/yr technology spend risks being diluted by engineering "busywork" and manual governance bottlenecks. By continuing to rely on disjointed toolchains and manual PR reviews, CBA sacrifices speed-to-market against agile neobanks and leaves compliance enforcement vulnerable to human error.

## 2. Core Operational Pains & The Atlassian Rovo Solution

### Challenge 1: Developer "Busywork" & Context Switching
* **The Pain:** Engineers waste thousands of hours searching for context across Jira and Confluence instead of writing feature code, slowing down release cycles.
* **The Solution:** Rovo lives natively in the IDE and CLI, instantly surfacing contextual knowledge and generating necessary boilerplate to keep developers in their flow state.
* **Expected ROI:** Immediate recovery of thousands of engineering hours annually, directly accelerating feature delivery.

### Challenge 2: Pull Request Bottlenecks
* **The Pain:** Manual code reviews consume expensive senior engineering talent and delay the DevSecOps pipeline in an environment where vulnerabilities are unacceptable.
* **The Solution:** Rovo automates first-pass code reviews and verifies alignment with Jira acceptance criteria before a human even looks at the PR.
* **Expected ROI:** Significant reduction in PR cycle times and recovery of senior engineering capacity for high-value architectural planning.

### Challenge 3: Inconsistent Code Standards
* **The Pain:** Enforcing strict compliance and coding standards manually across thousands of disparate developers relies on human oversight, leading to reliability issues.
* **The Solution:** Rovo automates compliance by enforcing your bespoke security and architectural standards directly at the point of code creation.
* **Expected ROI:** Dramatically reduced risk of security vulnerabilities and guaranteed code reliability across the entire enterprise ecosystem.

## 3. Why Atlassian Rovo? Why Now?
* **Strategic Advantage:** Only Rovo natively leverages the Atlassian tools CBA already uses (Jira, Bitbucket, Confluence) to provide AI agents with actual organizational context.
* **Cost of Inaction:** Delaying AI integration not only limits engineering productivity but allows more agile digital challengers to outpace CBA in customer feature delivery.
